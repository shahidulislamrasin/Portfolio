How I would help Phitron students

These are samples I made for this application to show how I would work. They are not from a past job.

Subject: Let’s get you back on track, one small step at a time

Hi [Name],

I noticed you haven’t been able to submit the last two assignments, and I wanted to check in. Falling behind happens to many learners, and it is completely fixable.

Here is a simple plan for this week:

  1. Re-watch only the [topic] module (about 40 minutes), nothing else.
  2. Solve the two easiest practice problems and share where you got stuck.
  3. Join our short call on [day/time] so we can go through the hard parts together.

If anything is unclear or something outside the course is getting in the way, just reply here. I am happy to help.

Warm regards,
Shahidul Islam
Student Relationship Team, Phitron

  1. WelcomeGreet warmly and let the learner talk first without interrupting.
  2. UnderstandAsk open questions (“Where exactly did you get stuck?”) and repeat back what I heard.
  3. Find the causeSeparate technical gaps, time problems and motivation problems.
  4. Agree a small planTwo or three concrete steps for the next week, with a date.
  5. Follow upSend a short summary by email and check progress at the agreed time.

Inactive

Example criteria: no login or class activity for 7+ days

Action: friendly check-in email, then a call

Falling behind

Example criteria: missed 2+ assignments in a row

Action: personal study plan and call

Repeated doubts

Example criteria: same topic raised in feedback or the Facebook group

Action: share the topic with the team; suggest a revision resource or session

  • A short “first week” checklist for new batches, so learners know exactly what to do.
  • A weekly Facebook post that answers the most common doubts from the previous week.
  • Small peer study groups for learners who are at the same stage.
